ABOUT THE COLLEGE

A foundation for your
healthcare journey.

Grace College of Medical Sciences is presented as a learning destination for students interested in healthcare support and diagnostic services. Our website is designed to help prospective students understand available courses, enquire about admission and connect with the college team.

We believe learning should combine clear concepts, responsible practice, professional conduct and respect for every patient.

What courses are listed on this website?+

The current website lists an OT Technician Degree Course and a Pathology Technician (DMLT) Degree Course. Confirm the exact official names and qualifications directly with the college.

What is the eligibility and course duration?+

Eligibility and duration depend on the approved course and awarding body. These details are intentionally not guessed here. Contact admissions for the current written criteria.

What documents should I prepare?+

Ask the college for its current document checklist. Commonly requested items may include prior academic marksheets, identity and photographs, but the official list must be confirmed by the institution.

Does the college provide placement or internship support?+

Ask the college to confirm its current placement, internship and clinical-training arrangements, including partner names and any eligibility conditions.

How can I apply?+

Submit the enquiry form on this page or use the official contact details after they have been added. The college should guide you through its verified application process.

Are these courses government-approved or degree programmes?+

Approval, affiliation and the qualification awarded must be confirmed using the relevant official documents and competent authorities. This website does not make an unverified recognition claim.
